WHAT IT MEASURES

Browser-exposed motion and orientation. Phones vary, permissions vary, and nearby electronics can affect physical sensors.

WHAT IT DOESN'T CLAIM

No sensor in a phone has been scientifically validated as a ghost detector. BEAROT presents paranormal tools as experiments and symbolic prompts, not proof.

ANTI-REPETITION

Words and tarot cards use shuffled decks. Oracle readings combine independent pools and recent combinations are suppressed.
